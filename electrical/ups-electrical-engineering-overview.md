# Data Center UPS Electrical Engineering Overview

This project demonstrates key electrical engineering concepts used in mission-critical data center power systems, with a focus on UPS architecture, electrical calculations, commissioning, protection, and troubleshooting.

## Technical Areas

- Critical power distribution from utility through switchgear, UPS, PDU, and IT loads
- UPS N+1 redundancy and capacity analysis
- Three-phase electrical calculations including voltage, current, kW, kVA, and power factor
- UPS electrical commissioning and functional testing
- UPS alarm and failure troubleshooting methodology
- Electrical protection and breaker coordination concepts
- BMS/EPMS-assisted electrical monitoring and fault analysis
- Critical power reliability and redundancy concepts

## Engineering Approach

This example demonstrates a structured approach to evaluating critical electrical infrastructure, including system loading, available capacity, redundancy, abnormal operating conditions, and UPS performance.

During commissioning and troubleshooting, electrical parameters, equipment status, alarms, one-line diagrams, and operating conditions can be reviewed to identify abnormalities and verify expected system performance.
